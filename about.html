About GMC Technology

Indian manufacturer of laboratory equipment and customized scientific systems.

GMC Technology is an Indian manufacturer and engineering partner for laboratory equipment, scientific instruments, scientific glassware, reactors, laboratory furniture and customized scientific systems for academic, research and industrial laboratories.

What GMC makes

A portfolio spanning equipment, glassware, furniture and custom engineering.

Its portfolio spans high-pressure reactors and autoclaves, photochemical reactors, bioreactors, thermal equipment, specialized instruments and a broad range of scientific glassware and assemblies.

The website organizes these capabilities into a clear product and application architecture.

The portfolio is complemented by configurable laboratory furniture, including workbenches, fume hoods, chemical storage, overhead storage and instrument workstations. Customization remains a central capability across equipment, glassware, furniture and integrated laboratory systems.

Vision & mission

Materials enabling scientific discovery.

GMC Technology's approach is centered on advanced glass, metal and ceramic technologies that support scientific research, innovation and industrial development, with an emphasis on equipment quality, customized scientific systems, safety and continuous improvement.
